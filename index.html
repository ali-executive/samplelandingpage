How it works

From ACN to diagram in three steps

Step 1

Search by name or ACN

Find any company on the register and choose a current extract, or current and historical when you need past officeholders.

Step 2

Review before it’s added

See every new entity and relationship first. Companies match on ACN and people on name and date of birth, so nothing doubles up.

Step 3

Present and export

Tiermap places each entity using the shapes advisers already use. Walk the client through it, then export a branded PDF or PNG.

Built for Australian structures

It knows a trustee from a shareholder

Shapes advisers expect

Companies are boxes, trusts and SMSFs are triangles, people are ovals. Ownership, trusteeship and beneficiaries each get their own line style.

Non-beneficial holdings, handled

When a trustee company holds shares for a trust already on the diagram, Tiermap links the shares to the trust and flags anything it can’t place.

ACNs checked as you type

Manual entries run the ASIC check-digit test, so a mistyped ACN is caught before it reaches a client file.

One diagram per client group

Combine extracts for every company in a group, then add trusts, SMSFs and partnerships the register doesn’t cover.

Your firm’s branding

Logo, colours and a footer line on every export, so diagrams sent to lawyers, planners and banks look like your work.

Laid out in seconds

Owners sit above what they own, lines avoid crossing where they can, and you can nudge any box by hand.
